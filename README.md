# Auction Theory Simulation

A Python-based simulation comparing first-price and second-price sealed-bid auctions under bidder risk aversion.

## Objective

The project investigates how bidder risk preferences affect bidding strategies and seller revenue across different auction mechanisms.

## Methodology

- Modelled bidder utility using CRRA utility functions
- Simulated 10,000+ auction outcomes
- Compared first-price and second-price auction mechanisms
- Analysed the impact of varying levels of risk aversion
- Visualised revenue distributions and bidding behaviour

## Key Findings

The simulations show how increasing risk aversion can lead bidders to bid more aggressively in first-price auctions, affecting expected seller revenue relative to second-price auctions.
